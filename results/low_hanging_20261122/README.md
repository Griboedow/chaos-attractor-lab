# Four follow-ups on existing attractors

Each result starts from a system and parameter set already in the atlas. The linked studies give the proof, a runnable check and the closest prior work found.

| Question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [Are the two rings chaotic inside?](annuli/README.md) | Yes, in the sense of positive topological entropy. Both connected components contain open sets. Within the outer component, two inverse branches encode every binary sequence. Which of those trajectories the plotted orbit approaches is unknown. |
| [How many periodic orbits can two circle reflections support?](algorithms/README.md) | One attracting set contains orbits of every least period 7n for n ≥ 7. At least 75,683,050 distinct orbits have least period 700. |
| [What happens to a straight geodesic when a wave amplitude changes?](geodesics/README.md) | The fifteen-handle surface has 24 straight closed routes. Changing one weight leaves eight. At weight 9/8, the shortest straight route has length 62.517, while one curved route closes in under 8.891. |
| [What captures the regular optical trajectories?](optics/README.md) | At one setting the optical map has a stable ten-cycle and two different period-two saddles. Very close starts near one saddle can enter the ten-cycle’s basin or spend a verified finite interval alternating between the field lobes. |

An additional lead concerned prescribed periods in polynomial Newton maps. A construction for every degree at least three and every period at least two was already in the literature. [Campbell and Collins, 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5687)
