# Icosahedral equilibrium and itinerary follow-up

Local outward interval certificates establish at least 161 distinct equilibria
for case 177 and 101 for case 183, with certified unstable-direction counts.
The root search is not exhaustive. Both 60- and 90-decimal checks are retained.

The numerical orbit visits thirty outer saddle-focus neighborhoods. Its 1,796
recorded transitions follow edges of their icosidodecahedral direction geometry.
Twelve finite perturbations near two saddle types retain positive leading rates.
These observations are not a proof of heteroclinic connections, a Markov partition,
asymptotic chaos, or historical first discovery.

The structural diagram explicitly marks equilibrium centers and straight guide
edges as annotations. The motion GIF uses a fixed camera and actual trajectory
time; its dim background shows the full cloud, and its playback restart is not
a recurrence of the dynamical system.
