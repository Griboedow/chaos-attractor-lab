# A certified fold belonging to the attracting RAAR set

The [mathematical statement and proof](theorem.md) combine the frozen trapping
certificate with a thinner self-covering window, opposite whole-face
determinant signs after 42 iterations, and a complete fold classification.
Historical uniqueness and noninjectivity restricted to K remain unproved.

The proof inputs are `runs/raar-critical-thin-window01/input.json`, both
`raar-critical-thin-cover*` runs, the three `raar-critical-thin-faces*` runs,
both `raar-critical-thin-fold*` runs, and accounting audits 03/04. The primary
and scalar automatic-derivative realizations use 70 and 100 interval digits.
Their enclosure driver and mpmath library are shared. Audit 02 establishes
membership without the later fold classification.

All 21 development runs are represented by retained sources, parameters,
summaries and certificates. Nine large proposal or duplicate arrays are kept
outside Git; `omissions.json` records their hashes, sizes, array descriptions,
cache locations and reproduction commands. They are not proof inputs. The
complete final figure data is retained as an essential audit input. The first doubleton attempt failed in a
numerical matrix-selection conversion; its source and failure record remain.
Audit 01 failed because Decimal negation rounded a stored endpoint during an
exact comparison; audit 02 fixes that accounting error. The wide-window fold
experiment was interrupted after the complete thin-window proof passed. Its
partial compressed output and recovered complete-record prefix are explicitly
not a complete partition or a proof. No failed attempt is counted as success.

The final diagnostic is `runs/raar-critical-membership-render02`. It retains
8,193 actual centerline images, determinants, camera and certified location
bounds. The centerline is not asserted to lie in K. The old symbolic and
trapping packets remain unchanged and are identified by the audit manifests.

Reproduction commands appear in [the companion article](../../papers/raar-critical-membership/README.md).
`manifest.json` binds every file; `provenance.json` records copied run bytes.
Third-party papers are cited, not redistributed.
