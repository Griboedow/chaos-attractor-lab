# Gaussian-posterior reflection: certified entropy and periodic structure

For **Case 156**, the maximal invariant set K in the previously certified
trapping region attracts a neighborhood and has topological entropy
**at least log(2)/6 > 0.11552 per iteration**. Seven interval-certified
crossings give arbitrary binary route sequences inside K. Independent
binary64 and 80-digit interval implementations verify every crossing.

Three 200-digit certificates, repeated with an independently differentiated
map, locate a fixed point with one unstable direction and two five-cycles
with two unstable directions. All belong to K. Their multipliers exclude
C1 neighborhood conjugacy to a dissipative planar Hénon map with a
contracting extra coordinate, and to a fixed-time map of an autonomous
three-dimensional flow. The exact scope and short proofs are in the article.

An exact spanning-tree certificate and an independent connectivity check
also prove that K is connected. Opposite orientation signs at its certified
periodic points force failure of local injectivity at some point of K.
Thus ambient neighborhood conjugacy to any locally injective map, including
invertible three-dimensional Hénon maps, is excluded even under continuous
coordinate changes. This does not prove noninjectivity of the restriction
to K; intrinsic equivalence remains unresolved.
