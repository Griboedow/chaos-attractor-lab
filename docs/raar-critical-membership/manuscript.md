# A Whitney fold in the attracting spatial-circle RAAR set

CHAOS ATTRACTOR LAB · RESEARCH PREPRINT · 1 NOVEMBER 2026

**Abstract.** We prove that a previously certified compact attracting positive-entropy set of the spatial-circle RAAR map contains a smooth Whitney fold of the full map. A thin self-covering window yields a connected crossing continuum inside the attracting set. Opposite whole-face signs of det DF after 42 steps force a critical point in its image. Exhaustive interval classification verifies the fold criterion at every possible critical point in that window image. Two map/derivative realizations and separate partition audits pass.

The fold excludes conjugacy on an open neighborhood of the attracting set, by any ambient homeomorphism, to a map locally injective throughout the corresponding set. This is a precise local-equivalence obstruction. It does not establish noninjectivity of the restricted invariant-set dynamics or a historically new attractor class.

```math
F(x)=\beta x+\beta P_A(2P_B(x)-x)+(1-2\beta)P_B(x).
```

## 2. Crossing continuum and membership

**Crossing lemma.** A crossing path in N is a continuous path whose endpoints lie on the opposite u faces. The inequalities in Section 1 imply that G maps any such path across N. More precisely, its unstable coordinate starts beyond one face and ends beyond the other. Select the subinterval from the last encounter with the starting face before the first encounter with the opposite face. Between these endpoints its unstable coordinate stays in [−1,1]. The volume condition puts both stable coordinates strictly inside, so this image subpath is a crossing path in N.

Start with a centerline path and repeatedly select a crossing subpath in the preceding path’s image under G. Every resulting path is compact, connected and meets both faces, with

```math
P_{n+1}\subset G(P_n)\cap N,\qquad P_n\subset G^n(N)\subset F^{7n}(Q).
```

Compactness of the hyperspace of nonempty compact subsets of N supplies a Hausdorff-convergent subsequence, with indices tending to infinity, and a nonempty compact limit C.

The limit is connected: otherwise disjoint small neighborhoods of its two separated compact pieces would both meet, and together contain, every sufficiently late P<sub>n</sub>, contradicting connectedness. The closed-face intersection property also passes to the limit. For every m, sufficiently late P<sub>n</sub> lie in the closed set F<sup>m</sup>(Q), since the forward images of Q are nested. Thus C⊂K∩N, and C crosses both faces. C is not asserted to be a smooth curve.

**Forced critical point.** The function h(x)=det DF(F<sup>42</sup>(x)) is continuous on N, because N⊂Q and the trapping certificate gives smoothness along all forward iterates. Whole-face interval enclosures establish the conservative bounds

```math
h(\{u=-1\})<-0.0311,\qquad h(\{u=1\})>0.1744.
```

The connected image h(C) contains values of both signs, hence contains zero. Choose x∈C with h(x)=0. Invariance of K gives z=F<sup>42</sup>(x)∈K and det DF(z)=0. This supplies membership in the asymptotic attracting set, rather than proximity to a finite reference orbit.

**Fold type.** Section 3 classifies the entire image F<sup>42</sup>(N). Every critical point in it has rank DF=2 and a kernel direction v with ∇(det DF)·v≠0. The critical hypersurface is smooth and its kernel is transverse; the standard Whitney-fold criterion applies [3]. At least one such point of K lies in the outward box

```math
[0.20984,0.22744]\times[-2.86973,-2.84328]\times[3.86195,3.88395].
```

The box encloses all fold-capable primary image cells. We do not locate a unique critical point, assert that the numerical centerline belongs to K, or infer that all points in the box are critical.

## 3. Exhaustive interval computation

The 42-step image of each source cell is enclosed by a reconditioned mean-value representation X⊂C+Aδ+Br. A and B are point matrices, while δ and r are interval vectors. For arbitrary finite selected matrices A′ and invertible B′, the mean-value theorem gives

```math
F(X)\subset F(C)+A^{\prime}\delta+E,
```

```math
E=(DF(X)A-A^{\prime})\delta+DF(X)Br.
```

An interval enclosure of (B′)<sup>−1</sup>E becomes the next remainder r′. A numerical QR factorization selects B′, but exact orthogonality or accuracy of this choice is not assumed: its actual inverse is enclosed and verified. The explicit mismatch DF(X)A−A′ absorbs the chosen linear approximation. Positive projection-axis bounds are checked throughout each convex enclosure.

Every source cell in N is accepted only if the final image X either has det DF(X) bounded away from zero, or passes the fold criterion at all possible critical points in X. In the second case, select two rows of DF. Their cross product v has a component bounded away from zero, certifying a nonzero 2×2 minor. The interval contraction ∇(det DF)·v also excludes zero. At a critical point, rank is then exactly two and v spans its kernel.

## 4. Equivalence, priority and reproduction

**Ambient obstruction.** At a smooth fold, separate source and target coordinate changes give the local form (s,t,w)↦(s,t,w²), up to signs and permutations [3]. Thus F is not injective on any open neighborhood of z. If an ambient homeomorphism H conjugated F to T on an open neighborhood of K and T were locally injective at H(z), the conjugacy identity and injectivity of H would force F to be injective on a sufficiently small neighborhood of z. This is a contradiction.

The nearby pairs witnessing the fold need not both belong to K. Therefore the argument does not show noninjectivity of F|K and does not rule out abstract conjugacies defined only on invariant sets. The fold normal form itself is not a dynamical conjugacy.

**A concrete known comparison.** The generalized Hénon map H(x,y,z)=(1.76−y²−0.1z,x,y) in [4] has an explicit global inverse. Our result excludes an ambient-neighborhood conjugacy to it. Its symbolic dynamics and hyperbolicity were already computer-assisted results; neither covering proofs nor a folded-looking portrait are new concepts.

Noninvertible folded strange-attractor candidates also precede this study [5]. The institutional abstract of [5] was reviewed; its full body remains an access gap. The present contribution is a specific fold-membership certificate in an instance of the known RAAR algorithm, with a precise equivalence exclusion. Historical priority, transitivity of K, a physical measure and equality of K with the sampled cloud remain unresolved.
