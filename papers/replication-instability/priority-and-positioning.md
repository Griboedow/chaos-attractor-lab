# Priority and positioning

Audit date: 30 October 2026. This document positions the manuscript's contribution; it does not certify historical priority.

## Precise contribution

An explicit five-cluster periodic orbit of the Alouges et al. first-harmonic motor model is enclosed and proved attracting in the full `N=8` system. Balanced replication at fixed normalized parameters creates unstable modes in formerly singleton groups. The complete Floquet index is `(2k-2, 1, 22k)` at `N=8k`; all stable multipliers are below `0.13`, and all unstable multipliers lie in `(-23.084,-23.083)`. The same finite block analysis also gives qualitative persistence in a common small parameter neighborhood.

The strongest novelty candidate is this **explicit, validated model-specific family and its complete index**, supported by a reproducible proof packet. The analytic decomposition uses established cluster-stability ideas. An unusual curve, a high-dimensional projection, or symmetry-related copies do not supply a separate novelty claim.

## Wording suitable for the manuscript

“We establish an explicit computer-assisted periodic-orbit and replication-stability result in the first-harmonic motor model.”

Avoid “the first cluster desynchronization mechanism,” “the first motor chaos,” “840 new attractors,” or “a proved strange attractor.” The manuscript does not use these claims. Completing the two thesis comparisons is the specific remaining priority check before stronger novelty wording is considered.
